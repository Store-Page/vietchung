Filmory — Film Camera Diary

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Viet Chung Company Limited ("Viet Chung", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Filmory - Film Camera Diary mobile application (the "App"). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Summary

Filmory is a local film-style photo diary. Your photos, edits, and diary entries are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers or tied to an account, because the App does not require or support account creation or sign-in. We do receive limited technical and analytics data from your device through the third-party services described below, mainly to keep the App stable, measure how features are used, show ads, and process in-app purchases.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is never transmitted to Viet Chung:

  • Photos and camera captures you take or import within the App.
  • Edits, filters, and diary entries you create in the App.
  • App preferences such as language and appearance settings.

Uninstalling the App, clearing its storage, or losing your device may permanently delete this data, since there is no cloud backup or account to restore it from.

3. Information collected automatically

We use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, and attribution SDKs that automatically collect limited technical data when you use the App, including:

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language, and time zone.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — screens viewed, features used, session length, and crash/error logs.
  • Advertising and attribution identifiers — the Android advertising ID (or a privacy-preserving alternative such as the Google App Set ID), install-referrer data, and similar identifiers used to measure ad performance and how you found the App.
  • Approximate, non-precise location — inferred from IP address, used only in aggregate for analytics and ad delivery; the App does not request GPS/precise location permission.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials, because the App has no account system.

4. Device permissions the App requests

  • Camera — to take photos and apply film-style effects in real time.
  • Microphone — only used if you record video/audio within a capture feature; not accessed otherwise.
  • Photos/Media (storage) — to import existing photos into the App and to save edited photos back to your device gallery.
  • Set wallpaper — only invoked when you choose to set a photo as your device wallpaper.
  • Notifications — to send optional reminders or updates about the App; you can disable this in your device settings at any time.

Each permission is requested only when the related feature is used, and you can grant or deny any of them from your device's Settings app.

5. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob (Google's Ads Mobile SDK), using Google's mediation platform to source ads from several advertising networks. Depending on your region and consent choices (collected through Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow), ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier and app usage, or non-personalized. The advertising and mediation networks integrated in the App are:

  • Google / AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Vungle (Liftoff Monetize) Privacy Policy
  • Mintegral Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads Privacy Policy
  • Pangle Privacy Policy
  • TikTok For Business Privacy Policy

We also use mobile measurement partners (MMPs) for ad measurement and install attribution:

  • Adjust Privacy Policy
  • AppsFlyer Privacy Policy

These providers may independently collect device and advertising identifiers, IP-based approximate location, and app-usage events under their own privacy policies linked above.

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier, or opt out of personalized ads, from your Android device Settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads / Google > Ads).

6. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config) to understand how the App is used, diagnose crashes, and safely roll out features. Firebase may process device identifiers, usage events, and crash/stack-trace data on our behalf. See the Firebase privacy and security page for details.

7. In-app purchases

If the App offers premium features or a subscription, purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Viet Chung does not receive or store your payment card details; Google's own Privacy Policy governs payment data handling. We only receive confirmation of purchase status to unlock features in the App.

8. How we use the information collected

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App's features and stability.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues.
  • To measure app installs, ad performance, and marketing campaign effectiveness.
  • To display and personalize ads, subject to your consent choices.
  • To process and validate in-app purchases.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited technical and advertising identifiers with the service providers named in this policy (Google/AdMob/Firebase, the ad mediation networks listed in Section 5, and our MMPs Adjust and AppsFlyer) solely so they can provide analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and attribution services on our behalf, under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Viet Chung, our users, or the public.

10. Data retention

Content you create in the App (photos, edits, diary entries) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Technical, analytics, and advertising data collected by our third-party providers is retained according to each provider's own retention policies, as referenced above.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

12. Your choices

  • Deny or later revoke any device permission (camera, microphone, photos, notifications) from your device Settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of ad personalization, from your device's Google Ads settings.
  • Uninstall the App at any time to remove locally stored content from your device.
  • Contact us using the details below to ask questions about this policy.

13. Security

We rely on reputable third-party providers (Google, Meta, and our mediation, MMP, and analytics partners named above) that maintain industry-standard safeguards for the data they process. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. We will update the "Effective date" above when changes are made. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
